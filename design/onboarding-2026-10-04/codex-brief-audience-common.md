# "What do you watch?" card art for Previously. (iOS) — brief for the image generator

The attached screenshot is the screen these pictures are for: three wide cards, each the PICTURE
of an answer — Anime, TV, Anime and TV. The art in the screenshot is REJECTED: flat one-colour
duotones that look cheap and dim. Replace it with art someone would stop and look at.

Generate the images listed below with your built-in image generation tool and save each as a PNG
at the exact path given (opaque, no transparency needed). Do not edit any other file. Do not write
code. When done, print each saved path with its pixel size.

## Every image

- **Wide landscape, 3:2 or wider, at least 1536 px wide.** The card shows the middle 2.1:1 band
  (the top and bottom ~15 % are cropped away), so keep everything that matters inside that band.
- **The subject lives in the RIGHT 60 %.** The bottom-left third is where the card prints its name
  in white type: keep it calm and dark there — real picture (never a flat fill), but no faces, no
  bright spots, no fine detail.
- **Full, rich colour with deep blacks** — it sits on a near-black (#09090B) app. Luminous
  highlights, saturated mid-tones, shadows that fall to black at the left and bottom edges. Never
  monochrome, never washed out, never a flat graphic.
- Cinematic and premium: one clear subject, strong light direction, atmosphere (haze, rain,
  embers, petals, light shafts), a sense of scale.
- **No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, borders, frames or UI.**
- **Original only**: no existing characters, franchises, studios or brands; no real people — any
  live-action person is fictional and resembles no actor or public figure.
