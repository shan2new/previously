# First-run artwork for Previously. (iOS) — brief for the image generator

Generate TWO original illustration assets with your built-in image generation tool and save each
as a PNG with a REAL transparent alpha background at the exact path given. Do not edit any other
file in this repository. Do not write code. After saving, print each saved path with its pixel
size and whether the corners are transparent.

The attached image is a MATERIAL AND STYLE reference (an existing asset of the same family, an
"empty Library" illustration). Keep its graphite hinged split-flap panel material, the warm ivory
mark and the single tiny coral accent — but draw the new subjects below, not episode frames.

Shared style (both assets): premium tactile 3D editorial miniature; matte charcoal graphite panels
(#292931 to #45454F) with subtle edge highlights so they separate from a near-black #09090B canvas;
soft warm ivory (#F4F1EC) for small marks; exactly ONE tiny coral-red (#F0563F) circular accent.
Near frontal with very slight perspective. One compact object, centred, modest transparent
padding. Broad simple shapes that read at about 128 by 100 points. Soft lighting. No background
rectangle, no ground plane, no glow, rays, bloom, sparkles, confetti, glossy bevels, fabric, paper
texture, beige or amber. No words, numbers, letters, logos, people, faces, animals, named shows or
other companies' marks.

## 1. `source/empty-home-flap-board-v1.png`  (landscape, about 5:4)
Subject: a small split-flap DEPARTURES BOARD — a compact graphite frame holding three short rows
of blank split-flap cells (each cell two hinged halves with one clean horizontal seam and tiny
axle pins at its sides). Every cell is blank; ONE cell in the top row is caught mid-turn, its upper
flap tilted forward a few degrees so the mechanism reads. One slim ivory right-facing play triangle
on the first cell of the top row; the single coral dot sits at the end of that row like a status
light. It should read at a glance as "a board waiting for its first listing" — not a calendar, not
a keyboard, not a chocolate bar, not a window.

## 2. `source/first-run-alert-bell-v1.png`  (square, 1:1)
Subject: a single notification bell built from the same matte graphite panel material — a simple,
slightly chunky bell silhouette with one clean horizontal seam across its body like a split-flap
hinge, small axle pins where the seam meets the edges, a rounded ivory clapper peeking below, and
the single coral dot at the upper right like an unread badge. Friendly and solid, readable at 56
points. Not a cartoon, not gold, not shiny.
