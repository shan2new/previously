
## The images (ANIME — hand-drawn Japanese animation key art)

Style for both: theatrical anime key visual — crisp cel shading on the character, clean confident
line work, a painterly, luminous background with real depth, cinematic rim light and bloom. It
must read instantly as ANIME (illustration), never as a photograph or 3D render.
Palette: warm — crimson, magenta, molten gold, with violet shadows.

1. `source/audience-anime-a.png` — **Rooftop at dusk.** A lone young swordfighter seen from
   three-quarter behind, standing on a rooftop ledge on the right, long coat and scarf whipped by
   wind, a sheathed blade across the back. Beyond: a vast city lighting up below and an enormous
   sky of towering clouds on fire with crimson, magenta and gold; a huge pale moon; drifting
   petals and embers catching the light. Small figure, big sky: scale and longing.
2. `source/audience-anime-b.png` — **The strike.** Close on a determined young warrior on the
   right, three-quarter view, hair swept by motion, eyes lit amber, a drawn blade crossing the
   frame catching a hard streak of light; sparks and embers fly; behind, a blurred night sky in
   crimson and violet. Dynamic diagonal composition, intense, heroic.
