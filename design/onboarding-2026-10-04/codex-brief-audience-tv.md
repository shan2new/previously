
## The images (TV — live-action television, photographic)

Style for both: a frame from prestige television — photorealistic live-action cinematography,
anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, real skin and fabric, practical lights, fine film grain.
It must read instantly as a PHOTOGRAPH from a series, never as an illustration.
Palette: cool — deep teal and steel blue, with warm amber practical lights for contrast.

1. `source/audience-tv-a.png` — **The detective.** Night, a rain-slick city street. On the right,
   a figure in a long dark coat seen from behind and slightly to the side, collar up, walking
   toward blurred neon signs and headlights that bloom into anamorphic bokeh; wet asphalt
   reflecting teal and amber; breath and rain in the light. Mystery, momentum.
2. `source/audience-tv-b.png` — **The throne room.** A vast, candle-lit stone hall at night. On
   the right, a cloaked figure seen from behind standing before tall windows where cold blue
   moonlight pours through haze; warm candles and braziers along the walls; banners in shadow.
   Epic scale, quiet tension — a fantasy drama, shot for real.
