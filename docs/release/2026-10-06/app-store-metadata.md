## Proposed customer-facing text

Subtitle: Your anime & TV, remembered

Promotional text: Pick up where you left off. Track anime and TV, bring your existing watchlist, and see what’s next in one calm, personal viewing room.

Description:

Your next episode. Always in reach.

Previously. keeps your anime and TV watching together, from the shows you’re following now to the ones you want to return to.

• Continue with confidence. See your next episode, update your progress, and undo a mark when you need to.
• Keep a library that makes sense. Track seasons and related installments together, with planned, watching, completed, paused and dropped lists.
• See what’s coming. Browse your schedule and choose local episode notifications.
• Bring your watchlist. Preview and import a public AniList or MyAnimeList list.
• Find your next favorite. Search anime and TV, explore genres, and discover recommendations.
• Return to a favorite. Keep a history of first watches and rewatches.
• Stay in control. Export your library, manage your settings, or request account deletion in the app.

Previously. is free for this release, with no subscription required. Full episodes are watched through your chosen streaming service. Catalog details, schedules and regional streaming availability can change.

Keywords: anime,tv,watchlist,tracker,episodes,seasons,schedule,shows,anilist,myanimelist,rewatch
