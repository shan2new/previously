## Saved review notes

Previously. is a free anime and TV watchlist and episode-progress tracker. No subscription or in-app purchase is required for this release. It does not host or stream full episodes. Catalog entries may link to regional streaming providers; trailers use the official YouTube player and may show contextual advertising.

For review, tap Sign in, enter the dedicated account email supplied in Sign-In Information, and tap Continue. Enter the supplied password and tap Continue. This account does not require a mailbox code or MFA for sign-in; its password login has been tested on the production build. If a previously opened screen offers an email code, choose Use another method, then Sign in with your password. No access to the operator's mailbox is needed. The account contains only synthetic viewing progress: Attack on Titan after episode 1, Reacher before episode 1, and a completed Death Note watch. Its library makes the core tracking features easy to review. Public comments, replies and chat are disabled for this release. Numeric user ratings remain available in aggregate.

To review tracking, open a title from Home or Library, mark an episode watched, and use Undo to reverse the change. Library supports planned, watching, completed, paused and dropped titles. Schedule shows upcoming episodes. Discovery supports anime and TV search. Settings includes library export and account deletion.

Imports accept public AniList or MyAnimeList watchlists and show a preview before applying changes. Catalog and schedule availability depend on the upstream providers. Explicit adult titles and titles tagged Hentai are excluded from consumer discovery and imports. Mature non-explicit content remains available and is reflected in the saved age questionnaire.

The production backend is deployed on the Mac Mini and will be kept available throughout review. Support and privacy information are available at https://previously.cognipin.com/support and https://previously.cognipin.com/privacy.
