# Spike — Apple access, deletion, UGC and privacy evidence

Checked 7 October 2026 using current Apple Developer pages and 2026 searches. This is research, not a legal opinion, compliance certificate or App Review reply. No account, credential, provider, runtime or metadata operation was performed. The governing case evidence is [Apple's actual six-part 2.1 information request for 1.0 (14)](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-07/app-store/rejection-dom.txt).

## Key findings

1. **The physical recording is specifically requested in this case.** Apple asks for the submitted app launched on a physical device running the latest OS, with applicable registration/login/deletion, UGC controls and paid access. Its message requires the same information in Reply and Review Notes. This is stronger than inferring a universal video requirement from **2.1(a)**, which requires device testing, usable demo access and a live backend. [Actual request](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-07/app-store/rejection-dom.txt), [Apple guidelines — 2.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#app-completeness).

2. **Password without OTP is a tested access choice, not a universal Apple rule.** Apple's current help requires working demo credentials and explicitly addresses authentication codes: supply the required code in advance through Notes, or review may need a call. No universal password-only exemption or prohibition of MFA was found in that guidance. Previously's dedicated reviewer has two recorded password logins without mailbox access; ordinary disposable-account Device Trust is a separate flow. Do not weaken ordinary-account security or expose private credentials in a video. [Apple complete-review help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review/), [tested reviewer route](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/native-live/review-otp-native-recheck.json), [saved private handoff proof](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/app-store/review-credentials-saved-receipt.json).

3. **Account creation triggers in-app deletion under 5.1.1(v).** Apple requires an easy-to-find route deleting the account and associated data, except legally required retention; deactivation alone is insufficient. Identity verification and confirmation are allowed if they do not make deletion unnecessarily difficult. Pending completion and retained information must be explained. Non-regulated apps should not force a support phone/email/chat journey. This does not prohibit a truthful pending-provider result. [Apple deletion help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion/). Previously's existing guide separates ready baseline, the exact disposable target, actual consumer Delete, and read-only post verification; it does not claim the pending live journey passed. [Physical recording guide](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/release/2026-10-07/physical-device-recording-guide.md), [guarded verification scope](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/native-live/live-account-erasure-verification.md).

4. **Missing Apple tokens must not prevent account erasure.** Apple's **TN3194**, first published **3 October 2025** and rechecked live now, says to fulfill deletion even without a refresh/access token or authorization code: erase account data, guide manual grant revocation, and handle revoked credentials. Programmatic revocation uses Apple's token endpoint. This supports a truthful manual fallback; it does not prove Previously's real Apple grant was revoked. [Apple TN3194](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3194-handling-account-deletions-and-revoking-tokens-for-sign-in-with-apple), [token revocation reference](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapplerestapi/revoke-tokens).

5. **Comments off narrows the experience; it does not establish a blanket 1.2 exemption.** **1.2** requires filtering, reporting with timely responses, abusive-user blocking and contact information for UGC/social services. Apple's age-rating definition concerns user content distributed broadly as part of the intended experience. Private watch history is distinct from public posts; aggregate numeric ratings/likes remain user contributions. I found no official exemption specifically declaring these aggregates outside 1.2. That classification remains Apple's judgment, not a research conclusion. [Apple 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content), [Apple age-rating definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions). Actual installed comments are disabled; their report/block controls are not exposed, while numeric participation remains. State that accurately rather than saying “no UGC” or pretending controls were demonstrated. [Installed flags](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-07/app-store/provider-config-readback.json), [route contract](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/server/src/routes/social.ts:76), [numeric response](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/server/src/routes/social.ts:596). Apple's **6 February 2026** update expressly clarified random/anonymous chat under 1.2; it does not resolve this ratings question. [Dated Apple update](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=d75yllv4).

6. **Privacy remains about actual data practices, including SDKs.** **5.1.1(i)** covers accessible policy, collection/use, third-party protection and retention/deletion. **5.1.2(i)** addresses disclosure and explicit permission for sharing personal data, including with third-party AI. Labels must include integrated partners and stay current. Editorial AI being configured alone proves neither personal-data sharing nor that every article uses AI. [Apple privacy rules](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#privacy), [Apple privacy-label help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy), [sanitized AI flags](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-07/app-store/provider-config-readback.json). Previously's four archived manifests, six-category Store label and effective policy have separate evidence; those disclosures do not prove live deletion. [Archive inventory](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/archive14/privacy-manifest-inventory.json), [published label](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/app-store/privacy-published-receipt.json), [effective policy](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/landing-final/effective-publication/qualification-receipt.json).

7. **Question 6 is conditional; protected material is independently relevant.** **5.2.1** covers permission for protected material; **5.2.2** covers permitted third-party service use and authorization on request. Neither is limited to paid apps. Complete-review help lists rights documents such as contracts or permission correspondence; it does not say every integration requires an individually negotiated contract. [Apple 5.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property), [Apple documentation guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/before-submitting-for-review/complete-review/). The Program Agreement's **3.3.4(A)(ii)** likewise distinguishes owned content from permission to use another owner's content. [Current Apple agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/). Previously displays provider metadata/artwork and embedded trailers; full episodes are not hosted, but that fact does not erase the protected-material question. The owner's saved content-rights declaration is an attestation, not evidence of a particular license. No agreement has been supplied in this research scope. [Owner declaration](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/qa/2026-10-06/app-store/owner-declarations-authorized.json), [actual response/source inventory](/Users/shan2new/Projects/previously/docs/release/2026-10-07/apple-review-response-draft.md). No regulated professional service was identified in that entertainment-tracker scope; this is not a determination of every jurisdiction's law.
